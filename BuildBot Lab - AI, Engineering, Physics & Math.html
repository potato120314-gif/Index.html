⚖️ Weight test

Strengths (kN): Foundation 100, Pillar 30, Wall 25, Anchor 20, Truss 14, Cable 10, Beam 8, Deck 6, Roof 2. Weight = mass × 9.81. This is a simplified model: load travels along connected parts and the weakest link sets the limit. Real engineers also check bending and buckling. More paths to the ground make a stronger structure.
